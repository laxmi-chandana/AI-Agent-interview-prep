# Buildathon Demo Script

## 30-second pitch
I built an AI interview coach that helps candidates practice for technical interviews in a personalized way. It generates role-specific questions, remembers the candidate's background, and gives actionable feedback after each answer.

## Product story
Most candidates prepare for interviews with generic question lists. This product changes that by simulating a realistic interview flow.

## Demo flow
1. Launch the app
2. Enter a candidate name and role such as backend engineer
3. Enter years of experience and strengths
4. The agent asks a realistic technical question
5. The user responds with a sample answer
6. The agent gives feedback in 5 parts:
   - what was good
   - gaps
   - what to improve
   - better answer outline
   - follow-up question
7. Show that the next round is personalized based on previous answers

## Key selling points
- local, low-cost AI with Ollama
- personalized memory across sessions
- realistic technical interview simulation
- strong hackathon MVP story
- easy to expand into a full product

## Judge-friendly explanation
This is not just a chatbot. It is a coaching product that adapts to the user's background and tracks their learning journey over time.
